Cozy Village & Home — Low Poly Props

Thank you for downloading this asset pack! This is a collection of 16 optimized low-poly props designed for cozy simulators, farming games, or casual RPGs.
Inside you'll find everything you need for basic indoor rooms and outdoor village scenes — from simple fences to a water well.

Setup & How to Use

Make sure your project is using the Universal Render Pipeline (URP).
Drop the package into your Unity project.
Open the Showcase Demo Scene to see the intended lighting and setup, or just drag prefabs straight from the Prefabs folder into your world.

Technical Details & Optimization

This pack is highly optimized for performance:
Zero Textures: The assets use Vertex Colors instead of traditional texture maps. This means zero VRAM usage, tiny build sizes, and near-instant loading.
Draw Call Efficiency: Everything shares a single master material (+ one for emissive parts), allowing for SRP Batching. You can fill a scene with these props and keep it down to just 1–2 draw calls.
Grid Snapping: All pivots are cleaned and placed at the absolute bottom/center of the models for easy layout.

Custom Vertex Toon Shader

This package includes a custom graphics shader and does not contain any C# scripts or runtime code, requiring no external offline documentation.
Included in the pack is a custom HLSL shader (Custom/URPVertexToon) built specifically for vertex-colored meshes. Select the main material in your inspector to tweak:
Lighting Style: Switch between Standard Lit and stylized Toon shading via a simple dropdown menu.
Toon Controls: Adjust the threshold, smoothness, and shadow intensity to fit your game's art style.
HDR Emission: Includes a dedicated toggle and multiplier for glowing elements.

Package Specs

Unity Version: 2021.3 LTS or higher (URP).
Polycount: Very low (from 48 triangles for the fence up to 1,734 for the well).
Rigging/Animations: None.
UV Mapping: None (Vertex Colors only).
