## 🎯 Usage in Unity

When creating `DeliveryPoint` objects in your scenes, you can copy the values directly into the Inspector:

```csharp
District Name:       Downtown_Center
Recipient Name:      Chef Marco
Address Name:        Downtown - City Bakery
Address Description: 2-story bakery with an awning and fresh bread baskets in front.
Required Level:      1
```
